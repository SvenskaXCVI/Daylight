## Description

Daylight helps families understand the day at a glance. See each child's school schedule, keep family calendar events separate and clear, manage tasks and groceries, check school-week weather, and prepare the morning drive.

Create a new private family or securely link to an existing Daylight device. Personal display names and appearance choices stay local, while shared family updates can synchronize between authorized devices.

Key features:

- Daily school schedules with leave-by and pickup information
- Family calendar, agenda, tasks, and grocery lists
- Weather guidance and school-route planning
- Important-event and reminder notifications
- Customizable liquid-glass appearance and accessibility controls
- Optional encrypted family synchronization
- Clean first-run setup with no preloaded personal information
